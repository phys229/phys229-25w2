# PHYS 229 Oral Exam Rubrics

## 2025 W2

A letter-grade-framed rubric used in a traditional cumulative points grading system.

| Grade | Summary | Reassessment |
|---|---|---|
| A+ (8/8) | Everything at A-level, plus deep, well-connected understanding and independent thinking, such as spotting where assumptions fail, generalizing, or proposing experimental tests. Rare. | None |
| A (7/8) | Correct on factual questions, with logical reasoning that explains *why*. Works through changed scenarios instead of guessing. | None |
| B (6/8) | Solid reasoning on most topics, with one or two weak spots or incorrect reasoning that needed prompting. | Can earn 6.5/8 (A-) through follow-up questions |
| C (5/8) | Most facts correct, but reasoning has gaps, wrong turns, or skipped steps. Understanding is patchy. | Can earn 6/8 (B) through follow-up questions |
| D (4/8) | Some facts correct, but reasoning is fragmented. Prompting leads to guessing or repetition. | Can retake; grade is the average of the two attempts |
| Inc (0–3/8) | Struggles to start, misses the point of questions, and shows little understanding of core ideas even with prompting. | Can retake; grade is the average of the two attempts |

| Grade descriptions |
|---|
| **A+:** Did everything at A-level, while also demonstrating an in-depth and well-connected understanding of the experiment. Also demonstrated signs of thinking beyond the specific questions, such as identifying where assumptions break down, recognizing opportunities to generalize, or proposing how to test a prediction experimentally. This level should be rare and genuinely impressive. It reflects a student who was not just answering questions well but actively thinking like a physicist. |
| **A:** Across the full exam, answered factual questions correctly and built coherent explanations on reasoning questions, where each step followed logically from the last. Explained *why*, not just *what*. When the examiner changed the scenario or pushed further on a reasoning question, worked through the consequences rather than guessing or otherwise making little progress. The overall impression was of a student who understood the physics, not just the facts. |
| **B:** Handled factual questions well and demonstrated solid, connected reasoning on most topics. Explanations generally held together, and follow-up probing from the examiner was handled well in most areas. However, there were one or two areas where incomplete or incorrect reasoning was given, or where substantial prompting was needed to make progress. The overall impression was of a student who generally understood the material but also had specific weak spots. |
| **C:** Handled most factual questions correctly. On reasoning questions, attempted to explain how things connect, but the explanations had gaps, wrong turns, or leaps that skipped over important reasoning. Follow-up or prompting questions revealed patchy and unreliable understanding. |
| **D:** Answered some factual questions correctly but missed many others. On reasoning questions, recognized some relevant concepts but was unable to fit them into the bigger picture. Prompting typically resulted in guessing or repeating what had previously been said. |
| **Inc:** Struggled to get started on most questions, or gave answers that missed the point of what was being asked. Answered few if any factual questions correctly, and on reasoning questions, even significant prompting did not help them demonstrate understanding of the core ideas. |

## 2024 W2

A coarse four-level rubric used with specifications grading.

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 4 - Exceeded Expectations | Showed a deep and robust understanding of the experiment and relevant physics. Able to answer all questions correctly and completely without prompting or hints. |
| 3 - Success | Showed a sufficient understanding of the experiment and relevant physics. Minor prompting and hints were required to be able to answer all questions correctly and completely. |
| 2 - Revise | Showed an adequate understanding of most of the experiment and relevant physics. The majority of the questions were answered correctly and completely, but significant prompting and hints were required. Follow-up questions will be provided afterward that need to be completed to earn Success on the Oral Exam. |
| 1 - Incomplete | Showed an inadequate understanding of the experiment and relevant physics. Many questions were answered incorrectly. A successful retake of the oral exam will be required to earn Success. |
